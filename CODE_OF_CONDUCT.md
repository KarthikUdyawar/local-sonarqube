# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming to everyone.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate.

Report violations privately to the maintainer at `karthikudyawar123@gmail.com`. All reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially.
